Cooling Process

How Does a Bulk Milk Cooler Work?

A BMC cools freshly collected milk and maintains it at a controlled temperature for temporary storage.

1

Milk Collection

Fresh milk is collected from dairy animals or milk collection points.

2

Milk Transfer

Milk is transferred into the stainless-steel cooling tank.

3

Cooling

The refrigeration system removes heat from the milk efficiently.

4

Controlled Storage

Milk is maintained at the required temperature until transportation or processing.
