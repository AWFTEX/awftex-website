About AWFTEX Industries

AWFTEX Industries is an India-based manufacturer, supplier, and solutions provider specializing in Dairy Equipment, Milk Processing Machinery, Bulk Milk Coolers, Milk ATM & Vending Machines, Water Solutions, Solar Solutions, LED Lighting, Electrical Systems, and Fire Safety Solutions.

We are committed to delivering reliable, hygienic, energy-efficient, and cost-effective industrial solutions for dairy farms, milk collection centres, dairy plants, commercial establishments, institutions, and businesses.

Our dairy equipment range includes Bulk Milk Coolers (BMC), Milk ATM Machines, Milk Vending Machines, Pasteurizers, Paneer Plants, Khoya Making Machines, Ghee Plants, Curd Incubators, Homogenizers and other dairy processing equipment.

With a customer-focused approach, technical expertise, and commitment to quality, AWFTEX provides end-to-end solutions from product selection and customization to delivery, installation, commissioning, and after-sales support.
