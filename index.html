About AWFTEX Industries

Engineering Reliable Solutions for Dairy & Industry

AWFTEX Industries is an India-based manufacturer, supplier and solutions provider serving dairy farms, milk collection centres, dairy plants, food businesses, commercial establishments and industrial customers.

India's Trusted Dairy Machinery Manufacturer

AWFTEX Industries is a manufacturer and service provider of milk processing and dairy equipment including Bulk Milk Coolers, Milk Pasteurization Systems, Ghee Plants, Paneer Plants, Curd Making Plants, Bulk Milk Chillers, Milk Cans, Khoya Making Machines, Vertical and Horizontal Steam Boilers, Curd Incubators, Hot & Cold Rooms and other dairy machinery.

We also support complete setup of new Dairy Processing Plants and Food Processing Plants through fabrication, erection, commissioning and EPC of MEP services.

Our Commitment

Our focus is hygienic design, dependable engineering, practical customization, efficient operation and responsive after-sales support. We help customers select suitable machinery according to capacity, process requirements, utilities and budget.
