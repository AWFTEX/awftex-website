AWFTEX provides integrated engineering and industrial solutions spanning dairy and food processing, industrial engineering, MEP, fire and safety, security, water and air, and energy and solar.

Project scope is defined around the requirement. Depending on the project, AWFTEX can coordinate engineering, equipment, installation and commissioning.
