Simple Operation

How Does a Milk Vending Machine Work?

A typical milk vending system follows a simple storage, selection and dispensing process.

1

Milk Storage

Fresh milk is stored in a suitable milk storage or cooling arrangement.

2

Customer Selection

The customer selects the required quantity according to the machine configuration.

3

Milk Dispensing

The machine dispenses the selected quantity through the dispensing outlet.

4

Customer Collection

The customer collects the milk in a suitable container.
